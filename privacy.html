Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Israel Radio Android app published by Arnav on Google Play. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

Radio streams

The app plays live audio streams operated by third-party radio stations. When you listen to a station, your device connects directly to that station's streaming server, which may receive technical information such as your IP address. We do not control those servers and do not receive that information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
